About the Institution

A college rooted in service, scholarship, and care.

Rameshwar Das Kedia Homoeopathic Medical College and Hospital is a medical institution dedicated to producing competent, ethical Homoeopathic physicians and providing accessible, evidence-informed Homoeopathic healthcare to the people of East Champaran and beyond.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality Homoeopathic medical education grounded in classical principles, modern scientific reasoning, and rigorous clinical training within an attached teaching hospital.

Our Vision

To stand among India's most respected Homoeopathic teaching institutions — known for academic discipline, dependable clinical care, and meaningful service to rural and semi-urban communities.

Core Values

Sincerity in learning, dignity in care, integrity in conduct, respect for the patient, and lifelong commitment to the Homoeopathic tradition and the public good.

Why this institution

A measured, disciplined approach to medical education.

Learning that begins at the bedside

A working teaching hospital, daily OPDs, and structured ward postings give students sustained exposure to real patients, real cases, and real clinical reasoning — supervised by senior Homoeopathic physicians.

  • Qualified, Stable Faculty

    A core teaching faculty across pre-clinical, para-clinical and clinical departments, supported by visiting consultants.

  • Well-Equipped Infrastructure

    Demonstration rooms, laboratories, museum, herbal garden, library, seminar hall and digital learning support.

  • Mentorship & Discipline

    Small-group mentorship, regular evaluation, and a culture that treats academic discipline as a clinical skill.

  • Service to the Community

    Regular rural health camps, public health awareness programmes and accessible OPD care for the people of East Champaran.

  • Ethical Practice

    Patient dignity, informed consent and honest record-keeping are taught alongside Materia Medica and Repertory.

BHMS Programme & Internship

A 4½-year Bachelor of Homoeopathic Medicine & Surgery programme followed by a 12-month compulsory rotatory internship — conducted as per NCH regulations.

Courses Offered

A structured undergraduate Homoeopathic medical programme.

The BHMS programme is conducted as per the regulations of the National Commission for Homoeopathy and the affiliating university, with mandatory clinical training in the attached teaching hospital.

Undergraduate · NCH-regulated

Bachelor of Homoeopathic Medicine & Surgery

B.H.M.S.

Duration
4½ years (academic) + 1 year compulsory rotatory internship
Curriculum
As notified by the National Commission for Homoeopathy (NCH) under the BHMS Course Regulations, 2022
Eligibility
10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Biology (PCB), minimum 50% aggregate (40% for SC/ST/OBC/PwD) and a valid NEET-UG score — as per NCH BHMS Admission Regulations
Age Limit
Completed 17 years on or before 31 December of the year of admission
Intake
As per the latest annual permission letter issued by NCH (Letter of Permission/LoP)
Admission Basis
NEET-UG merit through State Counselling (BCECEB, Bihar) / Central AYUSH counselling (AACCC)
Examinations
1st, 2nd, 3rd & Final BHMS conducted by the affiliating university as per NCH curriculum
Fee Structure
As notified for the academic session — see Mandatory Disclosures
Career Path
Clinical practice (after BHMS & State Board registration), hospital service, academia, public health and further studies

Compulsory Rotatory Internship

12-month structured internship as per NCH regulations

Awarded after passing all four Professional examinations. The intern is registered as a provisional medical practitioner with the State Board of Homoeopathic Medicine, Bihar, for the duration of training.

  • Practice of Medicine2 months · OPD & IPD
  • Repertory2 months
  • Materia Medica1 month
  • Organon of Medicine1 month
  • Surgery (Homoeopathic management)1 month
  • Obstetrics & Gynaecology1 month
  • Paediatrics1 month
  • Community Medicine / Rural Posting2 months · PHC / outreach
  • Pharmacy / Casualty1 month

Total = 12 months (52 weeks) of full-time clinical training, as prescribed by the National Commission for Homoeopathy. Detailed log books, attendance and rotation certificates are mandatory.

Home/Academics

Academic Departments

Departments built around the BHMS curriculum, supported by laboratories, museum, herbal garden and clinical postings.

Academic Departments

Departments built around the BHMS curriculum.

Organon of Medicine+

Foundational teaching of Hahnemannian principles, philosophy of cure, and case-taking methodology — the conceptual spine of Homoeopathic practice.

Faculty: Head of Department + lecturers · Support: Seminar room & case-discussion library

Homoeopathic Materia Medica+

Comparative and clinical study of Homoeopathic remedies, drug pictures, miasmatic relationships, and prescribing strategy.

Repertory+

Systematic case analysis using Kent, Boericke, Boenninghausen and modern computer repertorisation.

Practice of Medicine+

Clinical medicine across systems, integrated with Homoeopathic therapeutics and bedside teaching in the attached hospital.

Homoeopathic Pharmacy+

Pharmacy practical training, drug standardisation principles, and pharmacopoeial preparation of Homoeopathic medicines.

Anatomy+

Gross, surface and applied anatomy with dissection hall and well-stocked specimen museum.

Physiology & Biochemistry+

Functional understanding of the human body and clinical biochemistry essentials supported by laboratory practicals.

Pathology & Microbiology+

Disease processes, common laboratory investigations, and infection-related principles relevant to Homoeopathic clinical practice.

Forensic Medicine & Toxicology+

Medico-legal aspects of medical practice, toxicology, and the responsibilities of a registered Homoeopathic physician.

Community Medicine+

Public health, epidemiology, and the role of Homoeopathy in community-level preventive and primary care — supported by field postings.
